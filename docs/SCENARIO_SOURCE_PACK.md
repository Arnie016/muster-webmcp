# Muster scenario source pack

Status: research notes for a fictional training prototype. This is not an approved Emergency Response Plan, fire-engineering analysis, evacuation instruction, or substitute for a qualified professional.

## Official workflow translated into the product

SCDF describes a table-top exercise as a sit-down walkthrough of the Emergency Response Plan. The Fire Safety Manager introduces building-specific scenarios and anticipated problems, participants coordinate their roles and actions, and the exercise ends with a documented debrief and improvements.

Muster maps that workflow into six visible stages:

1. Select the approved-plan reference and exercise scope.
2. Read the role roster, fixture occupants, hazards, protection systems, and plan revision.
3. Start a fictional scenario and issue timed injects.
4. Record participant decisions, owners, uncertainty, and disagreements.
5. Check role, route, accounting, and assistance coverage.
6. Stage an after-action draft for Fire Safety Manager review and approval.

## Drawing information worth representing

The official evacuation-planning guidance says building plans can include site, elevation, and floor plans. Floor-plan details include escape and alternative routes; exit stairs; extinguishers; hose reels; rising mains; fire alarm panels and manual call points; fire-resisting construction; lifts; differentiated room and compartment use; fire-engine access; access panels; and unusual hazards or protected rooms. Drawings should be labelled, show use, and be reviewed when alterations occur.

The current fictional drawing therefore uses a title block, drawing number, revision, scale label, room use, dimension strings, route overlays, assistance marker, compartment boundaries, and example fire-protection symbols. It deliberately does not claim code compliance or approval.

## Exercise record fields

The product data model should preserve:

- drill date, time, simulated fire floor, participants, elapsed time, coordinator;
- fire warden and storey; fixture occupant names or pseudonymous IDs; present or absent status;
- storey evacuation status, cleared or not cleared;
- scenario, management response, evacuation procedure, participation, elapsed time, comments, conclusion, and attachments;
- observation versus assumption versus accepted improvement;
- assigned owner, due date, reviewer, and source plan revision.
