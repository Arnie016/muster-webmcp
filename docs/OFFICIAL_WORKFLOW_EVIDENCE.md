# Muster official workflow evidence

**Scope:** product evidence for a fictional workplace fire-drill training app, reviewed 4 September 2026. This is not an Emergency Response Plan (ERP), compliance checklist, equipment certification, or instruction for a live incident. Muster should rehearse an organisation's approved plan with synthetic or appropriately minimised training data; qualified people retain authority over real procedures and plan changes.

## 1. What the rehearsal should actually test

The first product rule is to label the exercise type. SCDF describes a **table-top exercise** as a scenario-led, sit-down walkthrough of the ERP by the Fire Safety Manager and Fire Safety Committee, without building occupants participating. It tests whether participants understand and can coordinate their roles, work through building-specific scenarios and injected problems, surface uncertainty, and identify plan updates. It does **not** prove that people, routes, alarms, or equipment performed in a physical evacuation. ([SCDF, Guidelines on Table-Top Exercise](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/fire-safety-docs/emergency-response-plan/erp-guidelines-on-table-top-exercise.pdf?sfvrsn=d19bc380_1), pp. 1–4.)

A physical drill can produce a different evidence set. The official sources support these narrow test targets:

| Evidence target | What evaluators should be able to observe | Defensible Muster record |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Objective and scope | A stated exercise objective tied to the approved plan and an evaluation criterion | exercise type, objective, ERP revision, evaluator, success criterion |
| Notification and communication | Whether the planned reporting, alarm/announcement, and communication arrangements are understood and observable, including accessible alerting | timestamped simulated notification events and evaluator observations, never a claim that Muster triggered or certified a system |
| Roles and coordination | Whether wardens, coordinators, and other nominated participants understand their assigned functions and can coordinate under scenario injects | role roster, decision log, inject, owner, uncertainty, hand-off |
| Evacuation plan | Whether assigned and alternative routes, floor status, assembly-area status, and building-specific constraints can be followed or assessed within the declared exercise mode | route considered, constraint, observed result, floor/assembly status, unresolved issue |
| Accountability and assistance | Whether the approved method accounts for workers and visitors and whether pre-planned assistance responsibilities are covered | fictional person or group, accounted status, assistance owner, location/status evidence, exception |
| Drill result | What occurred against the objective, including participation and elapsed evacuation time when these were physically observed | scenario, management response, evacuation procedure, participant total, observed time, comments and attachments |

This mapping is grounded in [SCDF's evacuation-planning stages and report fields](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/fire-safety-docs/emergency-response-plan/evacuation-planning-guidelines-%28approved-mar-2018%29.pdf?sfvrsn=228866eb_1) (pp. 8–10), its [Evacuation Drill Record Sheet](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/fire-safety-docs/emergency-response-plan/fsm_fep_evacuation_drill_annexf.pdf?sfvrsn=63cd5d56_1), [OSHA's EAP minimum elements and training guidance](https://www.osha.gov/emergency-preparedness/getting-started), and FEMA's requirement to align observations to exercise objectives in its [HSEEP evaluation workflow](https://preptoolkit.fema.gov/web/training/evaluation).

## 2. Accountability and assisted evacuation

**Accountability is a status chain, not a head-count badge.** OSHA's Emergency Action Plan rule requires procedures to account for all employees after evacuation; its planning guidance also includes visitors and other non-workers. SCDF's official records connect a floor warden and storey to occupant present/absent status, then roll floors up to cleared/not-cleared building status. Muster can therefore justify a drill-only chain of `person/group → floor register → warden confirmation → floor status → unresolved exception`. ([OSHA 29 CFR 1910.38(c)(4)](https://www.osha.gov/laws-regs/regulations/standardnumber/1910/1910.38); [SCDF Annex H, Floor Register](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/fire-safety-docs/emergency-response-plan/fsm_fep_floor_register_annexh2881abd9c47c4443a992e14c9d0e26ae.pdf?sfvrsn=41754e0f_1); [SCDF Annex I, Building Evacuation Status Chart](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/fire-safety-docs/emergency-response-plan/fsm_fep_building_evacuation_status_chart_annexi.pdf?sfvrsn=c393cc21_1).)

**Assistance must be pre-planned and assigned.** SCDF's disability guidance calls for identifying assistance needs, planned locations and allocated holding points; making arrangements for assistance; training designated staff; providing a communication path from holding points; and testing the procedures. OSHA likewise directs evacuation wardens to know the full layout and alternative routes and says training should cover people needing extra assistance and the buddy system. For Muster, the defensible fields are an exercise-safe person/group identifier, planned assistance need, assigned helper, planned location or holding point, communication check, observed status, and unresolved coverage. The app should not infer disability, choose a live route, or expose real medical details. ([SCDF, Fire Safety Requirements for Persons with Disabilities](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/fire-safety-docs/emergency-response-plan/erp-fire-safety-requirements-for-persons-with-disabilities.pdf?sfvrsn=9f44a146_1), §§3.2 and 8; [OSHA, Emergency Preparedness and Response: Evacuation Wardens](https://www.osha.gov/emergency-preparedness/getting-started).)

## 3. After-action review structure

Muster's output should be a **draft AAR/Improvement Plan for human review**, with this compact structure:

1. **Exercise identity:** exercise type, date, scope, approved-plan revision, participants, evaluators, objectives, and criteria.
2. **Scenario record:** scenario and inject timeline, participant decisions, and time-stamped observations or attachments. Keep observation, participant claim, and evaluator inference distinct.
3. **Analysis by objective:** expected plan behaviour versus observed exercise evidence; strengths; areas for improvement; accountability or assistance exceptions; and uncertainty still requiring review.
4. **Improvement plan:** each validated issue linked to a corrective action, responsible owner, target completion date, status, and closure evidence.
5. **Human review:** participant clarification, Fire Safety Manager or authorised reviewer decision, accepted ERP change if any, and comparison with prior drills. The agent may prepare and track; it should not approve the report or silently change the plan.

SCDF calls for an immediate, documented debrief covering observations, lessons, uncertainties, improvements, and proposed ERP changes; its evacuation-drill report adds scenario, management response, evacuation procedures, participation, time, comments, comparison, and attachments. FEMA's HSEEP workflow aligns evaluator observations to objectives, records strengths and areas for improvement, and moves selected gaps into tracked corrective actions. ([SCDF table-top debrief](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/fire-safety-docs/emergency-response-plan/erp-guidelines-on-table-top-exercise.pdf?sfvrsn=d19bc380_1), p. 4; [SCDF evacuation-drill report](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/docs/default-source/fire-safety-docs/emergency-response-plan/evacuation-planning-guidelines-%28approved-mar-2018%29.pdf?sfvrsn=228866eb_1), p. 9; [FEMA HSEEP evaluation](https://preptoolkit.fema.gov/web/training/evaluation); [FEMA HSEEP improvement planning](https://preptoolkit.fema.gov/web/hseep-resources/improvement-planning).)

## 4. Two film-safe official lines

Use no more than these two external claims, with the source and scope visible on screen:

1. **Singapore scope:** “SCDF requires at least 2 evacuation drills and 2 table-top exercises annually for premises requiring a Fire Safety Manager.” ([SCDF Emergency Response Plan hub](https://www.scdf.gov.sg/fire-safety-services-listing/emergency-response-plan).) This is a Singapore designated-premises requirement, not a universal cadence.
2. **Why rehearse:** “FEMA describes exercises as a controlled, low-risk way to evaluate capabilities and progress toward targets.” ([FEMA HSEEP Improvement Planning](https://preptoolkit.fema.gov/web/hseep-resources/improvement-planning).) This supports a training value proposition, not a safety-outcome claim.

**Claim boundary for the film:** do not say Muster makes a building compliant, improves survival, validates a live route, accounts for real occupants, or replaces an approved ERP, drill evaluator, Fire Safety Manager, or emergency service.
