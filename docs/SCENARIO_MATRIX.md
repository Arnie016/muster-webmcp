# Fictional scenario matrix

These are tabletop exercise templates, not physical fire models or emergency instructions.

| Scenario | Intended participant | Initial condition | Timed complication | Decision evidence | Debrief focus |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Office electrical room | FSM, wardens, security, CERT lead | Scripted detector activation beside a higher-hazard room | One stair becomes unavailable; assistance owner is absent | Route decision, floor accounting, named assistance owner | Role coverage, plan revision, elapsed coordination time |
| Hospital smoke-stop lobby | FSM, ward team, facilities, security | Scripted smoke report near one protected lobby | One patient cohort cannot use the assumed vertical route | Cohort status, horizontal/vertical strategy discussion, explicit clinical authority | Assumptions, mobility dependencies, unresolved professional questions |
| School science preparation room | FSM, teachers, operations, security | Scripted after-hours alarm in a restricted room | Contractor and student-group registers disagree | Reconciliation owner, zone sweep status, assembly accounting | Visitor data, handoff clarity, register freshness |

Only the office scenario is currently executable in the local MVP. The hospital and school rows are research-backed product specifications, not implemented or validated scenarios.
